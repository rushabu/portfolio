Learning Japanese has been a good reminder that progress is rarely dramatic. Most of it is repetition, small corrections, and trusting that the pieces will eventually click together.

That is part of why I enjoy it. The process feels familiar. Engineering also rewards patience, pattern recognition, and the willingness to look confused for a while before the structure becomes obvious.

## What it has taught me

- Consistency matters more than intensity
- Systems become less intimidating once you understand the rules underneath them
- Small daily effort compounds faster than occasional bursts of motivation
- Being a beginner again is good for humility

It is not directly a software project, but it still shapes how I learn, how I stick with hard problems, and how I measure progress.
