# Why Am I Learning Japanese?

Ever since 2020, I’ve been deeply interested in Japanese culture, language, and technology.

Like many people, it started with **anime and manga**. At first it was just curiosity — watching shows, reading manga, listening to Japanese songs, and noticing how different the culture felt compared to what I was used to.

But over time, that interest slowly expanded into something much bigger.

---

# More Than Just Anime

What initially attracted me was entertainment, but what kept me interested was everything beyond it.

I started exploring:

* Japanese culture and traditions
* the language itself
* technology and innovation in Japan
* work culture and education
* advancements in robotics and AI

The more I learned, the more I started thinking:

> “What if I actually go to Japan someday?”

At first, it was just a random thought.

Now it has become an actual goal.

---

# The Biggest Barrier: Language

There’s one major entry point to Japan that cannot really be avoided:

## The Japanese language.

Coming from a background of Hindi and English, Japanese felt completely different.

Not just the speaking style, but also:

* sentence structure
* grammar
* politeness levels
* kanji
* hiragana and katakana
* the overall way thoughts are expressed

At first it looked overwhelming.

Then it became interesting.

And eventually, it became something I genuinely wanted to learn seriously.

---

# Funny Enough, College Helped

I was already planning to study Japanese on my own or join classes at some point.

Then something unexpected happened.

Japanese became available as an elective in college.

So naturally, I chose it.

And honestly, that decision helped me a lot.

It gave me:

* a proper foundation
* structured learning
* consistency
* motivation to practice regularly

Instead of learning randomly, I finally had a system.

---

# My Current Goals

Right now, I have two major goals:

## 1. Score an A+ in Japanese in college

and

## 2. Clear the JLPT N5 with a good score

The long-term goal is even bigger:

* getting placed in Japan
* or pursuing my Master’s degree there

---

# Why Japan Specifically?

One of the biggest reasons is technology.

Japan has always been at the forefront of innovation, especially in:

* robotics
* automation
* embedded systems
* intelligent hardware
* AI integrated with edge systems

And honestly, that’s one of the areas I’m most interested in as a Computer Science student.

The idea of studying and working in an environment where advanced AI systems interact with real-world hardware is something I find genuinely exciting.

---

# Final Thoughts

Learning Japanese is not just about learning another language for me anymore.

It feels like:

* a long-term investment
* a challenge
* a gateway to opportunities
* and a way to connect with a country I’ve admired for years

Right now, I’m still at the beginning of the journey.

But hopefully, one day:

```text
日本へ行きます。

日本 - にほん (Nihon) - Japan
へ - え (pronounced “e”) - to
行きます  - いきます (ikimasu) - go
```

> “I will go to Japan.”
