I build software that is practical, thoughtful, and easy for people to use. I enjoy working at the intersection of product thinking, clean engineering, and clear communication.

Most of my work naturally pulls me toward AI systems, backend engineering, and products that solve a real operational problem instead of existing only as demos.

## Snapshot

- Based in Mumbai, India
- Interested in AI systems, full-stack development, distributed systems, and developer-focused products
- Currently exploring retrieval systems, privacy-preserving ML, and production-grade applied AI

## What I Like Building

I enjoy projects that have a clear user outcome, a meaningful technical challenge, and room for polish. That usually means products with reliable backends, clean interfaces, useful automation, or data-driven features that solve a real problem.

## How I Work

I care about clarity, ownership, and momentum. I like breaking complex work into understandable steps, writing maintainable code, and shipping improvements that genuinely help the people using the product.

## Outside of Work

Outside of building software, I enjoy technical communities, workshops, side projects, and learning for its own sake. That curiosity is a big part of why I like exploring things slightly outside my comfort zone, whether that is a new system architecture or even learning Japanese.
