FrameSift is a multimodal video analysis pipeline designed to reduce the number of frames that need human review in news broadcast footage. It combines frame differencing, histogram-based shot boundary detection, blur scoring, face detection, and OCR fusion using EasyOCR and Tesseract. The project also includes a full Tkinter desktop GUI, modular architecture, and automated tests, making it a strong blend of computer vision depth and software engineering discipline.
