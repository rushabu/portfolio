This project studies whether machine learning can infer the nature of encrypted computation by observing metadata side channels around Fully Homomorphic Encryption workflows. By analyzing features such as execution time, ciphertext size, multiplicative depth, and noise budget, the system demonstrates how a classifier can identify encrypted computation patterns without breaking the cryptography itself. It also explores defensive noise injection to reduce attack accuracy.
