Hospital Shift Scheduling tackles a classic NP-hard optimization problem using constraint-based reasoning and heuristic search. It considers staff availability, coverage constraints, shift preferences, fairness, and consecutive shift limits to generate strong weekly schedules. The project also adds a visualization and evaluation layer so schedule quality can be analyzed against both hard and soft constraints.

GitHub: [Hospital-Shift-Scheduling](https://github.com/rushabu/Hospital-Shift-Scheduling)
