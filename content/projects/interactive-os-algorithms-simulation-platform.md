This educational simulator covers classic operating systems algorithms including CPU scheduling and page replacement, with live visualization and side-by-side comparison modes. What makes it valuable is that it is not just demonstrative but genuinely usable as a learning tool, letting the same workload be observed under multiple algorithmic strategies in real time. It is also one of the most audience-facing repositories in the portfolio.
