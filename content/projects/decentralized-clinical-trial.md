A research-grade federated learning system for privacy-preserving clinical trials across 14 simulated patient nodes. It combines DP-SGD, Byzantine-robust aggregation, asynchronous gossip communication, validator-style consensus, and consent-aware checkpointing to explore how collaborative learning can work without centralizing sensitive healthcare data. It sits at the intersection of distributed systems, cryptography, federated learning, and healthcare AI.
